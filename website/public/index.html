A LOOK INSIDE NAGI
THE LITTLE THINGS ARE THE BIG THINGS

A lighter footprint.

Display-aware transitions, an optional 60 Hz efficiency mode, incremental search indexing and sampling that follows visible widgets.

Your PC. Your business.

No account or activity telemetry. Preferences stay on your PC. Weather and update checks connect only for their features.

Hello. Bonjour. こんにちは.

A drawn Nagi / ナギ welcome. 40 offline language packs, including Japanese, Chinese, Korean, English, French and Arabic. Add yours with JSON.

Open to possibilities.

Open source, built with WinUI 3, Win2D and .NET. Explore the code, add a translation or help shape what comes next.

BUILT AROUND WHAT YOU DO

A mode with
a little purpose.

Your profiles do more than arrange shortcuts. Choose an integrated mode, apply its widget layout, and connect the services you use.

PLAY, WITH LESS IN THE WAY

Gaming mode

Automatic game recognition for Steam, Epic and GOG. PresentMon FPS, available temperatures, active game time and Discord desktop running status.

  • Executable rules and locked profiles keep priority.
  • Optional tracing helper when Windows requires permission.
  • Apply a compact layout that hides distracting widgets.
KEEP YOUR PROJECTS CLOSE

Developer mode

GitHub notifications, pinned repositories and the latest Actions build status alongside CPU/RAM readings and your development shortcuts.

  • Public repository builds work without a token.
  • Connect your own token for notifications and private repos.
  • Read-only requests; refreshes follow visibility.
A LITTLE STUDIO AT THE EDGE

Creator mode

Recording and streaming status, a live microphone meter, current media and the existing OBS record, stream and three-scene controls.

  • Explicit OBS WebSocket connection.
  • Microphone peak sampling stops when its meter is hidden.
  • Nagi reads levels without recording your audio.
ONE THING AT A TIME

Focus mode

A current task, your timer and the next meeting from an HTTPS calendar feed or local .ics file. Only important Nagi notices get through.

  • Calendar sources are encrypted for your Windows user.
  • Daily/weekly recurrence, exclusions and edited meetings.
  • Other apps’ Windows notifications remain under your control.

Modes work with existing profiles: Work uses Developer, Stream uses Creator. Custom profiles can choose any mode. Connections are optional and missing data is shown clearly.
