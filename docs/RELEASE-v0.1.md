# Nagi v0.1

A glass desktop companion for Windows 11. Your apps, music and useful information, tucked into the edge of your screen.

The release is named **Nagi** and uses the **`v0.1`** tag. See the [v0.1 changelog](../CHANGELOG.md), [app guide](USER-GUIDE.md), and [dock/teardrop screenshots](WELCOME-AND-LAYOUT.md#teardrop-dock-and-app-folder).

Downloads: **Nagi-Portable.exe** for direct launch, **Nagi-win-x64.zip** for an extracted portable folder, or **Nagi-Setup.exe** for a per-user installation. SHA256 checksums accompany every package. Windows 11 22H2 or newer, x64; runtimes are included. The app and wrappers are unsigned.
