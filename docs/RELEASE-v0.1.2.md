# Nagi v0.1.2

A glass desktop companion for Windows 11. Your apps, music and useful information, tucked into the edge of your screen.

- Separate activity and error logs, including full exception details, app version, and theme/update events. Open them in Settings → About & diagnostics.
- Outline symbols for supported Windows apps, native installed-app icons for the other dock/search apps, and outline music-service links.
- Better light-mode contrast, live theme colors, and consistent quick-search/acrylic themes.
- Automatic update detection with the changelog, Update now and Later. Later keeps the app running; a verified download remains in Settings → Updates.
- Retry after a temporary network failure, verified downloads, preserved preferences and install/restart.

Downloads: **Nagi-Portable.exe**, **Nagi-win-x64.zip**, or **Nagi-Setup.exe**, each with SHA256 checksums. Windows 11 22H2 or newer, x64; runtimes included. Nagi remains in v0.1.x until the owner approves v1.
